Our Story

Built around tooling and manufacturing.

B.S.J Tooling Technology Limited was established in 2010 and is dedicated to developing and manufacturing injection molds for automotive, electronics, smart home and healthcare applications.

Our production base is located in Dongguan, an important industrial city in China. The company profile describes a factory covering approximately 10,000 square metres and a team of around 120 full-time employees.
