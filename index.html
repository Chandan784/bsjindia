Who We Are

Engineering quality into every detail.

Established in 2010, B.S.J Tooling Technology Limited focuses on the development and manufacturing of injection molds. Our production base is located in Dongguan, China.

With a factory area of approximately 10,000 square metres and around 120 full-time employees, BSJ serves customers across automotive, electronics, smart home and healthcare industries.
